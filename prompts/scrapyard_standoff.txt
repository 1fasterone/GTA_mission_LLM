TITLE
Scrapyard Standoff

GENRE
A third-person action survival game set in a junkyard. Genre is shooter — the
player fends off waves of scavenger enemies while scrounging for supplies.

STYLE
Gritty, rust-and-dust industrial junkyard. Stacked scrap-metal husks, chain-link
fences, oil-drum barricades, sodium-lamp orange lighting at dusk. Desaturated
palette with warning-tape yellow accents.

CORE PREMISE
The player is trapped in a fenced scrapyard as scavenger raiders close in at
night. Scrounge scattered parts, hold the barricaded gate, and survive until dawn.
A wounded mechanic trapped in the workshop radios in tips and pleads for backup
between waves.

PLAYER
A survivor with a scrap-built weapon. Third-person controller (move, sprint,
jump, aim, fire). Rigidbody physics, health system, ammo-limited weapon.

ENTITIES
- Scavenger: an aggressive raider enemy that rushes the barricade (kind: npc).
- Mechanic: a wounded NPC quest giver sheltering in the workshop (kind: npc).
- ScrapPile: a collectible pile of salvage parts (kind: collectible).
- BarricadeGate: the chain-link gate the player defends (kind: prop).
- OilDrum: an explosive barrel that can be shot to damage nearby enemies (kind: prop).

NPCS
- Mechanic: a voiced NPC sheltering in the workshop. He greets the player, warns
  about the next wave, and offers a choice of repairing the gate or scavenging for
  ammo first. Branching dialogue with a typewriter reveal.

AI
Scavengers use navmesh_patrol AI that shifts to a direct rush once they spot the
player, converging on the barricade gate.

SCENES
- Main Menu: play, settings, quit.
- Scrapyard: the single survival arena — workshop, barricade gate, wreck piles.
Scene transitions use a fade between menu and the scrapyard.

HUD
Health bar, ammo counter, wave counter (wave 1 / survived), and an objective
prompt near the Mechanic ("Repair the gate" / "Scavenge for parts").

AUDIO
Clanking scrap and rattling chain-link as enemies climb the fence, a scrap-metal
weapon's dry mechanical fire, a booming oil-drum explosion, a low tense ambient
wind drone, and a pulsing synth combat music loop that intensifies each
wave. Spatial audio so approaching scavengers can be heard before they're seen.

FEATURES
Save/load high wave count (json_file). Pause menu (resume, restart, quit to menu).
Wave spawner for scavengers. Impact/muzzle-flash effects on hits and the oil-drum
explosion.

VISUAL STYLE
Realistic-leaning stylized, URP with volumetric dust and flickering sodium-lamp
pools of light. Grimy metal textures, warning-tape yellow highlights.
