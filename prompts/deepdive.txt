Deep Dive — a 3D underwater exploration and survival game for PC.

The player is a deep-sea researcher exploring a collapsed underwater research station
in the Pacific Ocean. Armed with a harpoon gun and a rebreather, they must collect
data logs, survive predators, and escape before their oxygen runs out. The ocean
environment spans five distinct zones with separate Unity scenes.

SCENES (8 total):
1. MainMenu       — animated title screen with ocean caustic background, Start / Load / Quit
2. Shallows       — sunlit entry zone, coral reef, tutorials, small fish schools
3. KelpForest     — dense kelp canopy, low visibility, first shark encounter
4. WreckInterior  — dark flooded corridors of the research station, data log collectibles
5. AbyssalPlain   — pitch-black deep zone, bioluminescent creatures, boss shark encounter
6. EscapeRoute    — flooded escape tunnel with rising sediment, timed sequence
7. InventoryDive  — inventory and equipment management screen
8. SurfaceEnding  — ending cutscene: player breaks surface, rescue helicopter arrives

NARRATIVE — eight story beats:
1. Descent Begin  — player enters the water for the first time (on_start)
2. First Contact  — player spots a shark shadow in the kelp (on_enter_zone)
3. Signal Found   — player discovers a distress beacon in the wreck (on_pickup)
4. O2 Warning     — O2 drops below 25 percent, warning alarm triggers (on_timer)
5. Shark Ambush   — shark charges from the darkness (on_enemy_death)
6. Core Breach    — reactor room floods completely (on_enter_zone)
7. Boss Dive      — giant great white guards the escape hatch (manual)
8. Surface Break  — player reaches the surface (on_enter_zone)

SCRIPTS — every system the game needs:
- SwimmingController   (player)      3D six-axis underwater movement, buoyancy drift,
                                     current push, sprint bubble burst, surface bob
- O2Manager            (custom)      oxygen tank countdown, refill from O2 stations,
                                     low-O2 screen vignette + heartbeat, death on empty
- HarpoonGun           (custom)      ranged underwater weapon: charge, fire, reload,
                                     harpoon projectile physics, rope retract animation
- SharkAI              (enemy)       NavMesh patrol → alert → charge → bite state machine,
                                     reacts to player light and bubbles, flees on harpoon hit
- FishSchool           (custom)      boids-style flocking for small tropical and deep fish,
                                     scatter on player proximity or shark presence
- UnderwaterCamera     (camera)      Cinemachine third-person, lazy follow with fluid lag,
                                     depth-based fog colour shift, caustic overlay, tilt on roll
- GameManager          (game_manager) singleton: state machine (Menu/Playing/Paused/Dead/Win),
                                     score (data logs collected), lives (O2 refills remaining),
                                     scene transitions, save/load checkpoint
- UIManager            (ui_manager)  HUD: O2 bar (top-left), depth meter (top-right),
                                     harpoon ammo counter, collected logs counter,
                                     low-O2 pulse warning overlay, pause panel, game-over panel
- AudioManager         (custom)      spatial audio pool, music crossfade between zones,
                                     ambient layering (water, bubbles, creak, pressure),
                                     plays cues from AudioBank by id
- HealthSystem         (health)      player and shark HP, damage events, death callbacks
- DataLogCollectible   (collectible) pickup triggering story beat dialogue and score
- WaypointNavigator    (custom)      leads player toward next objective with a glowing fish guide
- InventoryManager     (custom)      equipment slots: harpoon, torch, sonar, O2 tank upgrades
- SaveLoadManager      (saveload)    JSON checkpoint save between scenes
- DialogueManager      (dialogue_manager) story beat subtitles and voice-over trigger
- MaterialPalette      (material_palette) runtime ocean colour palette application
- ProceduralSeabed     (level_generator) ProBuilder seabed topology: rocks, ridges, sand dunes

ENTITIES:
- Player: deep-sea researcher in dive suit, humanoid rig
- Enemy:  great white shark, generic rig, patrol + charge animations
- Vehicle: none
- NPC:    small tropical fish school (boid swarm, no rig, simple mesh)
- NPC:    giant manta ray (slow patrol, ambient, no combat)
- Collectible: data log canister (glowing green cylinder)
- Collectible: O2 refill station (wall-mounted tank)

AUDIO — twelve cues for a rich underwater soundscape:
- rebreather_bubbles   music    looping  ambient bubble stream from rebreather, constant
- ambient_deep_drone   ambient  looping  low sub-bass ocean pressure hum, eerie and vast
- ambient_shallows     ambient  looping  bright water shimmer, surface light, gentle current
- kelp_creak           ambient  looping  kelp stalks swaying and structure groan
- shark_approach       sfx              deep low pulse, danger proximity cue
- harpoon_fire         sfx              hiss and bubble trail as harpoon launches
- harpoon_reload       sfx              mechanical click and air hiss of reload
- fish_scatter         sfx              rapid small splashes as fish school disperses
- o2_alarm             sfx              rising beep cadence, low oxygen warning
- data_log_pickup      sfx              sonar ping with digital confirm tone
- boss_theme           music    looping  intense orchestral tension, boss arena
- surface_break_sting  music            triumphant swell as player reaches the surface

PROCEDURAL GEOMETRY:
