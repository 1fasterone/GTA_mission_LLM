A rooftop infiltration above the Vinewood hills. The player starts on the street
outside a guarded mansion and needs to reach a penthouse balcony overlooking the
party below to plant a listening device. Climbing up the fire escape in full view
of the guards would blow the whole job, so the player instead uses a grappling
rig that instantly hauls them from street level straight up onto the rooftop the
moment they trigger it.
