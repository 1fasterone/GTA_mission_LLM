TITLE
Turbo Toys 

GENRE
A fully 3D arcade racing game. Genre is racing — a kart racer / grand
prix with power slides, boost pads, and lap-based circuit races.

STYLE
Bright, colorful cartoon toy world. Chunky low-poly toy cars with big rounded wheels,
cel-shaded look, sunny saturated palette. Think toy cars racing across a playroom
brought to life.

CORE PREMISE
The player drives a cartoon toy car in a grand prix against three rival toy cars.
Race three laps of each circuit, drift through corners for a boost meter, hit springy
boost pads, grab scattered coins, and cross the finish line first. Rivals rubber-band
and taunt you. Win the cup across a season of tracks.

PLAYER
A cartoon toy race car. Uses a vehicle_car arcade controller (accelerate, brake,
steer, handbrake power slide, boost). Rigidbody wheel-collider physics with big
cartoon wheels. Third-person vehicle chase camera that swings wide on drifts.

ENTITIES
- PlayerCar: the player's cartoon toy race car (kind: vehicle / car).
- RivalRedRacer: an AI rival cartoon race car, aggressive (kind: vehicle / car).
- RivalSportCar: an AI rival cartoon sports car, fast on straights (kind: vehicle / car).
- RivalTrophyTruck: an AI rival cartoon off-road car, cuts corners (kind: vehicle / car).
- Star: spinning pickup collectible scattered on the track (kind: collectible).
- BoostPad: a springy trigger pad that launches the car forward (kind: prop).
All four cars are toy cars and should resolve to real Sketchfab toy car
models ( race car, low poly sports car).

NPCS
- Sparky the Pit Chief: a voiced NPC quest giver at the garage. He greets the player,
  explains the controls, hands out the next cup challenge, and barks race commentary
  (countdown, overtakes, final lap). Branching dialogue with a typewriter reveal.

AI
Rival cars use waypoint_follow racing-line AI around the track with rubber-band catch-up.

SCENES
- Main Menu: play, best times, settings, quit.
- Garage: talk to Sparky, pick your car, start the grand prix (hub scene).
- Sunny Speedway: the first circuit — a looping oval through a cartoon toy town.
- Beach Circuit: a second circuit along a sunny cartoon coastline.
Scene transitions use a fade between menu, garage, and each track.

HUD
Speedometer, lap counter (lap 1/3), race position (2nd/4), coin count, boost meter,
and a minimap of the circuit. Countdown "3-2-1-GO!" banner at race start.

AUDIO
Revving cartoon toy engine loop, squealing cartoon tire skids on power slides,
bouncy bumper crashes, springy boost-pad launches, bright coin-pickup chimes,
a cheering cartoon crowd, and an upbeat bouncy race music loop. Spatial engine
audio per car so you hear rivals around you.

FEATURES
Save/load best lap times per track (json_file). Pause menu (resume, restart, quit
to menu). Main menu and game-over / results screen showing finish position.

VISUAL STYLE
Cel-shaded cartoon, URP with bloom and bright directional sunlight. Rounded toy
geometry, thick outlines, playful colors.
