GENRE: motocross racing
STYLE: 2D side-scrolling, hand-drawn dirt-track aesthetic, warm dusk palette
CORE PREMISE: a lone motocross rider guns a dirt bike down a single scrolling ridge-line course, launching off wooden ramps between elevated dirt platforms to beat rival racers to the finish banner before the sun drops behind the hills.

REDLINE RIDGE is a 2D side-scrolling motocross racing game for PC. The camera tracks the rider rightward along one continuous lane as the dirt bike guns down the race track, carrying speed into wooden launch ramps that fling it across gaps onto the next elevated dirt platform. Missing a landing costs speed and health; nailing one keeps the throttle pinned. Two rival riders launch from the same gate and fight for position across every jump the whole way to the finish banner.

Bike handling is lean-based: throttle and brake control ground speed, and holding back or forward mid-air tilts the bike's nose for a clean landing on the next platform. A health bar tracks crash damage from a botched landing or a rival's bump; three crashes end the run. A boost meter fills from clean jump chains and rival draft, spent for a burst of speed on the final ramp. Checkpoint flags along the course bank the rider's split time, and a finish-line sequence freezes the standings the moment the leader crosses the banner.

The course is staged in layers behind the action: a distant mountain ridge crawls past slowest, a mid-ground pine tree line drifts by faster, and the near chain-link fence and hay-bale barriers snap past fastest of all, so the layered backgrounds themselves sell the sense of forward speed even when the camera holds the rider centered. Full dynamic lighting carries the race from hazy dawn light at the start gate through golden dusk by the final ramp — direct light rakes low across the dirt, each bike throws a long moving shadow across the platforms, a rider's headlamp kicks on for the shaded final stretch through the pines, and every landing kicks up a dust cloud rim-lit by the low sun. Volumetric god-rays cut through the pine trunks over the mid-course jump section.

Key systems this course needs: a health system tracking crash damage and a three-crash fail state; a pickup system for engine and suspension upgrades scattered along the course that improve boost fill rate and landing forgiveness; a trackside announcer NPC with voiced callouts at each checkpoint and at the finish banner; rival-rider AI that lines up jumps, drafts the leader, and bumps for position on narrow platforms; particle VFX for roostertail dirt spray off the rear wheel, headlamp dust motes, and a spark burst on a crash; a dissolve-and-glow shader on each checkpoint flag as it banks a split; a Timeline-driven slow-motion finish-line sequence when the leader crosses the banner; and fade transitions between the garage, the start gate, the race track, and the podium.

Render pipeline: URP with bloom for the low dusk sun and volumetric lighting through the pine trunks, on Unity 6000.0. Visual style is a warm hand-drawn dirt-track palette — sun-baked ochre dirt, faded denim-blue sky, rust-red ramps. Recommended packages: Cinemachine, Input System, Timeline, and Animation Rigging for the rider's lean pose.
