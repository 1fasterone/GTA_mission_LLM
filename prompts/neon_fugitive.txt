Neon Fugitive — a third-person open-world cyberpunk bounty-hunter game for PC.

The player is a rogue AI enforcement agent navigating six districts of a rain-soaked
megacity at night. Each district has its own visual identity: neon markets, industrial
docks, corporate towers, underground tunnels, a rooftop skyway, and the flooded
outer slums. All six districts plus a main menu, a boss arena, and a credits scene
must be separate Unity scenes.

NARRATIVE — eight acts of escalating story beats:
1. Activation: player boots up for the first time in an alley (on_start)
2. First Mark: player receives the first bounty contract (on_enter_zone)
3. Street Chase: player pursues mark through market crowds (on_enemy_death)
4. Betrayal: handler reveals the player is also a target (on_timer)
5. Underground: player escapes into the tunnel district (on_enter_zone)
6. Data Heist: player steals identity files from corporate tower (on_pickup)
7. Showdown: final confrontation with the original programmer (manual)
8. Escape: player reaches the sky-bridge extraction point (on_enter_zone)

AUDIO — rich layered soundscape across eight tracks:
- Pulsing synth main theme (looping music, plays on menu)
- District market ambient (looping ambient, tense urban crowd)
- Industrial dock drone (looping ambient, machinery hum and water)
- Chase adrenaline track (looping music, fast-paced synth percussion)
- Corporate tower tension (looping music, slow arpeggiated suspense)
- Rain and thunder environment (looping ambient, outdoor weather)
- Combat hit and gunshot SFX pack (non-looping sfx, action punctuation)
- Victory sting and credits outro (non-looping music, emotional resolution)

PROCEDURAL GEOMETRY — runtime-built world pieces:
- Procedural terrain for the outer slum district (terrain type, resolution 64)
- Procedural road network grid for market district (road type, resolution 48)
- Procedural water plane for the flooded outer zone (water type, resolution 32)
- Procedural room layout for the corporate tower interior (room type, resolution 24)

SYSTEMS — everything this game needs:
- Third-person player movement with parkour (wall-run, vault, slide)
- Cinemachine third-person camera with lock-on targeting mode
- Enemy AI with NavMesh patrol, chase, and alert states
- Bounty system tracking multiple live contracts simultaneously
- Health and damage system with shields and healing pickups
- Dialogue manager playing all eight story beats as in-world hologram events
- District manager switching ambient audio and sky color per zone transition
- Day-night cycle controller driving scene lighting and NPC schedules
- Vehicle controller for rideable hoverbikes in open sections
- Weapon system managing ranged and melee attack combos
- Inventory and equipment screen
- Minimap radar displaying bounty targets and district boundaries
- Save and checkpoint system between acts
- ProBuilder level generator producing the tunnel and rooftop districts at edit time

VISUAL STYLE: stylized — heavy neon on deep dark backgrounds.
COLOR PALETTE name "NeonFugitive":
