IRON FRONTIER — 3D third-person action-survival game

SETTING
A ruined industrial city. The player is a scavenger named Kael who must raid a heavily
fortified enemy compound, steal an experimental fuel cell, and escape by vehicle before
rival factions close in.

PLAYER
Third-person character controller with run, crouch, roll, and aim-walk stances.
Gamepad and keyboard+mouse input both fully supported.
Kael uses a stamina-gated dodge and can vault low obstacles (Rigidbody 3D physics,
kinematic character body, trigger zones for vaulting).
Health: 120 HP, regenerates 15 HP/s after 4 seconds out of combat. Armour layer
absorbs 30% of damage before health is touched.

CAMERA
Cinemachine third-person follow with soft-lock aim assist. Switches to a cinematic
dolly cut when Kael enters a vehicle.

HUD
Live health bar (armour overlay), stamina bar, ammo counter (current/reserve),
minimap (top-right, marks enemies and objectives), interaction prompt (centre-bottom:
"[E] Open / Pick up / Talk"), score panel tracking compound-raid score.

INVENTORY
Slot-based (8 slots). Kael starts with a pistol and a medkit. Lootable crates scatter
the compound. Weapons: pistol, SMG, shotgun, EMP grenade. Consumables: medkit, armour
shard, stamina boost.

ENEMIES & AI
Three enemy archetypes:
  • Guard (humanoid, navmesh patrol → chase → cover shooter when alerted)
  • Drone (flying swarm, patrols corridors, alerts on sight)
  • Heavy (humanoid, slow FSM guard, suppressing-fire behaviour)
Enemies are wave-spawned from four radial spawn points around the compound exterior.
First wave: 3 guards. Every 60 s a new wave adds 2 more enemies (continuous spawner).

INTERACTABLES
  • Compound gate: lever puzzle (two levers must be thrown in sequence)
  • Locked doors: triggered by keycards found in the environment
  • Fuel-cell terminal: readable terminal with a 3-line lore entry, then pickup
  • NPC mechanic "Sable": talks Kael through the escape, gives a bike key

DIALOGUE
Sable has a branching conversation tree (3 nodes). Player can ask about the compound
layout (reveals minimap markers) or go straight to the escape plan. Typewriter reveal,
skip-able.

AUDIO
Layered ambient: wind + distant machinery. Music: tense layer fades in when enemies
are alerted, swells on boss wave. Spatial SFX: gunshots, footsteps (surface-aware),
vehicle engines. 3D positional audio for enemies (hear before you see them).

VEHICLES (Blender models, all four bases)
  Kael escapes via three vehicle options she finds in the motor pool:
    • Tactical jeep   (base=car,   ride=0.35, wheel_r=0.72, bed=0, wheel_pairs=2)
    • Cargo truck     (base=car,   body_len=5.0, wheel_pairs=3, ride=0.2)
    • Recon plane     (base=plane, fuselage_len=6.5, wing_span=8.0, fin_h=1.4)
    • Dirt bike       (base=bike,  suspension=1, engine=1, tank=1, pedals=0)
  Each vehicle uses a vehicle_car controller variant, has its own chase camera,
  engine audio loop, and Rigidbody wheel-collider physics.
  Humanoid enemies are spawned as rigged humanoid base models (base=humanoid,
  height=1.85, shoulder_w=0.52).

MENUS & SCENES
  Scene 1 – Main Menu: play, load game, settings (audio sliders, invert-Y, rebind),
            quit.
  Scene 2 – Compound (main level).
  Scene 3 – Escape corridor (streamed in via async additive load, trigger-door
            transition when Kael reaches the motor pool).
  Pause menu available at any point in scenes 2-3: resume, save, settings, quit
  to main menu.
  Game-over screen on death with retry / load last checkpoint.

SAVE SYSTEM
Autosave on checkpoint trigger (entering the compound, reaching Sable, reaching the
motor pool). Three manual save slots accessible from the pause menu. Save data
includes: scene index, inventory contents, enemy wave number, Sable dialogue state.

HEALTH SYSTEM (enemies)
Guards: 60 HP. Drones: 25 HP (one shotgun blast). Heavy: 180 HP with armour (60 HP
shield that must be broken first). All enemies have a ragdoll-on-death transition.

VISUAL STYLE
Gritty low-poly with strong directional lighting. URP with post-processing: bloom,
chromatic aberration on damage, vignette. Day is overcast; escape sequence at dusk.
