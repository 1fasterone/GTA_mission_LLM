TITLE: Platform Four Lost Property

GENRE: a calm sorting and paperwork puzzle, played entirely through the
interface.

STYLE: warm and cluttered, hand-lettered card labels, brown paper tags, a green
desk lamp, the look of a real back office at eleven at night.

LOCOMOTION: none

GAME TYPE: isometric

PLATFORM: PC

RENDER PIPELINE: URP

CORE PREMISE
You are the clerk on the late shift at a city railway station's lost property
office. There is no character to steer and nowhere to walk. The world of the
game is the counter in front of you, the three labelled shelves behind it, and
the claim log you write the night into before you go home. Every action is
either a drag with the mouse or a word typed at the terminal, and both do
exactly the same thing to the office.

SCENES
1. NightCounter — the whole game: counter, shelves, terminal and claim log
   under one green desk lamp.

ENTITIES
- LostUmbrellas: the umbrellas handed in tonight, still wet, five of them
  (kind: pickup)
- LostSuitcases: three suitcases, one of them very heavy (kind: pickup)
- LostPhones: four phones, all switched off (kind: pickup)
- UmbrellaShelf: the tall narrow rack the umbrellas belong on (kind: prop)
- SuitcaseShelf: the deep low rack the suitcases belong on (kind: prop)
- PhoneShelf: the shallow tray of pigeonholes the phones belong in (kind: prop)
- ClaimLog: the running record of everything booked in tonight. It is written
  by typing at the terminal and cannot be dropped into by hand (kind: prop)
- LedgerTerminal: the keyboard and screen at the end of the counter, where the
  night's entries are typed (kind: prop)

MISSIONS
1. Clear the Counter — every lost thing goes on the shelf that matches it.
   - Shelve the wet umbrellas on the umbrella shelf (collect: LostUmbrellas x5)
   - Shelve the suitcases on the suitcase shelf (collect: LostSuitcases x3)
   - Shelve the phones in the phone shelf pigeonholes (collect: LostPhones x4)
2. Book the Night In — write down what you shelved.
   - Record every shelved kind in the claim log
3. Close the Office — stamp the log and go home.
   - Stamp tonight's claim log and turn off the desk lamp

NARRATIVE
1. The porter tips the day's finds onto the counter and leaves. (on_start)
2. Shelve every umbrella, suitcase and phone on the shelf that matches it.
   Nothing goes on the wrong shelf — the labels are the whole point of the job.
   (on_collect)
3. Book the shelved things in: record each kind in the claim log by typing at
   the terminal. The log is written, never dropped into. (manual)
4. Stamp the night's claim log and close the office. (manual)

FEATURES
pointer input, drag and drop, typed commands, save

AUDIO

- shelf_slot          sfx      oneshot  a thing settling into its labelled place
- refused_move        sfx      oneshot  the soft thud of a thing that does not
                                        belong there
- ledger_keystroke    sfx      oneshot  a key struck on the terminal
