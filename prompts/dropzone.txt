DropZone — a 3D skydiving simulation and stunt game for PC.

The player is a competitive skydiver chasing world records and adrenaline across five
iconic jump locations. Beginning aboard a propeller aircraft at altitude, they freefall
through open sky, execute formation manoeuvres with AI skydivers, deploy their parachute
canopy at the critical moment, and navigate to a precision landing pad. A wingsuit mode
unlocks after clearing the first three locations, allowing sustained glide along cliff
faces and through narrow canyon slots before chute deployment.

SCENES (8 total):
1. MainMenu         — animated title: skydiver silhouette against sunset cloud layer,
                      camera pulls back from altitude, Start / Career / Settings / Quit
2. AircraftInterior — player and AI formation team preparing inside a Cessna Caravan,
                      pre-jump briefing panel, door opens on cue
3. ColoradoFreefall — open Rocky Mountain jump, blue-sky daylight, formation objective
4. CoastalCliffs    — wingsuit slot between sea cliffs above crashing Atlantic surf
5. StormFront       — jump through a thunderstorm at dusk, lightning, turbulent air
6. CanyonRun        — wingsuit run through a narrow desert canyon at sunrise
7. NightJump        — full darkness, city lights below, glow-stick formation markers
8. FinalApproach    — cinematic landing: player steers canopy into championship arena,
                      crowd noise, trophy presentation

NARRATIVE — eight acts:
1. First Jump       — tutorial descent with radio coach, calm clear sky (on_start)
2. Formation Lock   — all four team members reach the formation diamond (on_enter_zone)
3. Canopy Tangle    — a team member's chute malfunctions, emergency cut-away (on_timer)
4. Storm Entry      — jump ship hits turbulence, door rips open early (on_enter_zone)
5. Lightning Strike — near miss with lightning bolt during StormFront jump (on_timer)
6. Canyon Wall      — wingsuit grazes a canyon wall, close call (on_enter_zone)
7. Record Attempt   — final formation with four AI divers in NightJump (manual)
8. Championship Win — canopy landing bullseye in the arena (on_enter_zone)

SCRIPTS:
- FreefallController   (player)      6-axis body attitude control in freefall:
                                     barrel rolls, tracking dives, delta position, flare;
                                     drag simulation scaling with body angle to airspeed;
                                     terminal velocity plateau at ~200 km/h; altitude HUD readout
- WingsuitGlide        (custom)      lift/drag ratio control, pitch/roll glide adjustments,
                                     proximity cliff detection, slot-gap navigation,
                                     transitions cleanly into FreefallController on exit
- ParachuteSystem      (custom)      deployment at configurable altitude, canopy inflation
                                     physics, toggles for left/right steering risers,
                                     flare brake for precision landing, reserve chute trigger
- AltimeterManager     (custom)      real-time altitude from aircraft exit to ground,
                                     pull-altitude alarm at 1500m, hard-deck at 600m,
                                     emergency auto-deploy below 300m
- FormationAI          (enemy)       AI skydivers fly NavMesh-free intercept curves to
                                     player-relative formation slots; break-off on pull signal;
                                     ragdoll on collision; celebrate on lock
- SkyCamera            (camera)      Cinemachine: freefall mode tracks player from 3m above
                                     and behind, smooth in tumbles; canopy mode orbits wide;
                                     ground-approach mode cuts low for landing drama
- GameManager          (game_manager) state machine (Menu/Briefing/Aircraft/Freefall/
                                     Canopy/Landed/Crashed/Victory); score = precision landing
                                     distance + formation time; career progression unlocks
- UIManager            (ui_manager)  HUD: altimeter tape (left), airspeed dial (right),
                                     formation proximity indicators (corners), pull-warning
                                     flash overlay, canopy steering diagram on deployment
- WindSystem           (custom)      procedural wind layers at different altitudes: jet stream
                                     high, thermals mid, gusts low; turbulence during StormFront;
                                     pushes player and AI formation alike
- LightningSystem      (custom)      StormFront scene: lightning bolt spawner, strike audio
                                     and screen flash, player repulsion force on near-miss
- AudioManager         (custom)      spatial audio pool, crossfade between altitude layers,
                                     doppler on rushing air, muffled inside aircraft
- HealthSystem         (health)      player survivability: canopy tangle = life lost,
                                     cliff graze = life lost, hard-deck breach = death
- FormationTracker     (custom)      measures formation slot accuracy and lock duration,
                                     scores each jump segment, writes to career save
- SaveLoadManager      (saveload)    career JSON: locations unlocked, best scores, wingsuit flag
- DialogueManager      (dialogue_manager) radio coach voice lines during briefing and freefall,
                                     eight narrative acts as in-ear transmissions
- MaterialPalette      (material_palette) runtime sky gradient palette: sunrise canyon warm,
                                     day blue-sky crisp, storm grey-green, night city-glow
- ProceduralTerrain    (level_generator) ProBuilder landscape: cliff ridges, canyon walls,
                                     landing-pad approach strip, crash terrain

ENTITIES:
- Player:       lead skydiver in jumpsuit and helmet, humanoid rig, freefall + canopy poses
- Enemy:        formation AI skydivers (3), humanoid rig, intercept behaviour
- NPC:          Cessna Caravan aircraft, slow loop above jump zone
- Collectible:  altitude gate ring (fly through for bonus score)
- Collectible:  formation lock target (diamond marker, all four divers must reach)
