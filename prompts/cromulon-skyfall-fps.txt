TITLE
Cromulon Skyfall

GENRE
A fully 3d first-person shooter. Genre is fps — an arena shooter with real
gunplay: the player holds a blaster in view, aims down the centre of the
screen, and fights waves of enemies that close in from every direction.

STYLE
Deep-space horror-comedy spectacle. The arena is a lone floating steel platform
adrift in a starfield with no ground below it — just black space, drifting
meteors and a distant nebula. The enemies are colossal disembodied heads:
enormous stone-coloured faces the size of buildings with huge lidded eyes that
drift silently through the void and stare at the player before they strike.
Harsh white platform floodlights, deep blue rim light, long hard shadows across
the deck, weightless dust motes.

CORE PREMISE
The player is an astronaut in a sealed space suit, stranded on a floating
platform in deep space, armed with one blaster. Giant floating Cromulon heads
drift in from the surrounding void in waves. Each head hovers, locks its eyes
on the player, and lunges to ram them off the deck. The player strafes around
the platform, shoots the heads in the eye to break them, and survives as long
as possible. Every cleared wave brings more heads and faster ones. Falling off
the platform edge, or being rammed once too often, ends the run.

PLAYER
An astronaut in a full white space suit with a domed helmet — the player
character model is an astronaut. First-person view: the camera sits at the
astronaut's head, mouse look drives the aim, WASD strafes across the deck.
Uses a first_person controller — CharacterController-based walk and strafe with
a short hop, held to the platform by gravity so walking off the edge drops the
player into space. The blaster is held in view at the bottom right of the
screen; left click fires a bolt from the muzzle straight down the crosshair
with a muzzle flash, a recoil kick and a short cooldown. Limited ammo per clip
with a reload.

AI
Each Cromulon uses a hovering chase AI: it drifts in from a random point on the
edge of the void, bobs slowly in place while it acquires the player, then
accelerates in a straight ram toward the player's position and overshoots past
the platform before circling back for another pass. Heads take damage anywhere
but take heavy damage on an eye hit. Elite heads acquire faster, ram harder and
take more hits. Waves spawn on a timer with a short lull between them; the
head count and speed step up each wave.

SCENES
- Main Menu: start run, high scores, settings, quit.
- The Deck: the arena scene — one floating steel platform in open space,
  railings along part of the edge, a generator and scattered crates for cover,
  meteors drifting past, and giant Cromulon heads inbound from the dark.

HUD
Centre crosshair that flares on a hit, suit integrity bar, oxygen bar, ammo
counter with clip and reserve, current wave number, kill count, a wave-incoming
banner, damage vignette at the screen edge when the player is hit, and a
game-over screen showing waves survived and heads destroyed.

AUDIO
Muffled in-suit breathing that speeds up as suit integrity drops, a punchy
blaster fire crack with a whine tail, a reload clack, a deep sub-bass drone
that swells as a head closes in, a wet crunch on an eye hit, a low chime on
wave clear, and a slow ominous synth drone bed. Spatial audio on every head so
the player can hear which direction the next ram is coming from.

FEATURES
Save/load high scores and best wave (json_file). Pause menu (resume, restart,
quit to menu). Game-over screen with a restart option.

VISUAL STYLE
URP in deep space with bloom on the blaster bolts, muzzle flash and platform
floodlights. Near-black background with a dense starfield, cold blue ambient
fill, hard white key light from the floodlights, heavy contrast and long
shadows across the deck.
