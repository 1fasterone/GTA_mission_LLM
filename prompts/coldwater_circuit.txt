TITLE
Coldwater Circuit

GENRE
A fully 3D, third-person arcade racing game. Genre is racing — a winter rally
circuit racer where the player drives a rally car against three rival cars on
frozen lake courses. Ice grip, not top speed, decides who wins.

CORE PREMISE
The player drives a lightweight rally car across four frozen-lake circuits in a
single winter championship. Every surface is ice with a different grade of grip:
polished black ice, wind-scoured blue ice, packed snow, and slush near the shore
where the lake is thawing. Studded tyres bite into snow and skitter on black ice,
so the racing line is a constant trade between the fast polished lane and the
slow surface that will actually hold a corner. Rivals learn the player's line and
block it. Warming huts along the shore act as checkpoints; passing one refills
the tyre-stud meter, and studs wear down every time the car slides.

SCENES (5 total):
1. MainMenu        — title over a frozen lake at first light, static aurora on the
                     horizon, Race / Championship / Settings / Quit
2. ServiceTent     — heated service tent hub between rounds: pick the car, choose a
                     stud compound, talk to the crew chief, start the round
3. BlackIceBay     — opening circuit on polished black ice, overcast flat daylight,
                     lowest grip and the widest drifts of the championship
4. SnowdriftPass   — packed-snow circuit threading between drifts and pine banks at
                     dusk, high grip, tight technical corners
5. ThawShoreFinal  — championship decider along a thawing shoreline at night under
                     floodlights, slush puddles that grab a wheel and pull the car

NARRATIVE — six acts:
1. Cold Start      — crew chief walks the player through studs and grip on a warm-up
                     lap of Black Ice Bay (on_start)
2. First Blood     — player takes a podium place off a rival for the first time
                     (on_enter_zone)
3. Stud Failure    — studs wear to nothing mid-race and the car goes loose on ice
                     (on_timer)
4. Blocked Line    — lead rival starts deliberately defending the fast polished lane
                     (on_enter_zone)
5. Thin Ice        — a shortcut across untested ice cracks under the car near the
                     shore (on_timer)
6. Championship Won — player takes the flag at Thaw Shore under floodlights
                     (on_enter_zone)

SCRIPTS:
- IceHandling          (player)        rally car handling on ice: per-surface grip
                                       coefficients for black ice, blue ice, packed
                                       snow and slush; weight transfer into a slide;
                                       counter-steer recovery; handbrake pivot
- StudWearSystem       (custom)        tyre-stud meter that depletes on every slide
                                       and refills at a warming hut; grip scales with
                                       remaining studs; warning chirp under 20 percent
- RivalRacerAI         (enemy)         three rival cars that learn the player's racing
                                       line over laps and move to defend the fast lane;
                                       per-rival aggression and recovery from spins
- SurfaceGripMap       (custom)        samples the track surface under each wheel and
                                       reports the grip grade to handling and audio
- ChaseCamera          (camera)        third-person chase rig that widens on a slide and
                                       tightens on the straights, speed-scaled FOV
- RaceDirector         (game_manager)  lap counting, checkpoint order, race position,
                                       countdown start, championship points across rounds
- WarmingHutCheckpoint (collectible)   shoreline hut checkpoints: refill studs, split
                                       time readout, respawn anchor after a spin
- RaceHUD              (ui_manager)    speed, lap 1/3, position 2nd/4, stud meter, split
                                       delta to the rival ahead, minimap of the circuit
- WinterAudio          (audio_manager) surface-aware tyre audio bed, engine load layers,
                                       wind across the open lake, muffled cabin interior
- CrewChiefRadio       (dialogue_manager) crew chief radio calls: stud warnings, rival
                                       position, thin-ice alerts, championship standings
- IcePalette           (material_palette) runtime palette swap per circuit: overcast grey
                                       bay, dusk blue pass, floodlit night shore
- FrozenLakeGenerator  (level_generator) procedural frozen-lake circuit: ribbon course,
                                       pine banks, snowdrift walls, shoreline slush patches
