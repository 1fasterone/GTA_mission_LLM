TITLE
VectorCore: Full-Surface Control Stack

GENRE
A 3D hyper-reactive physics sandbox / control-testing simulation. There is no
single objective genre like racing or platforming — the point of the game is
testing controller scripts, input action maps, mode switching, and physics
blending across every locomotion type the player can occupy: on-foot running
and parkour, driving cars/bikes/off-road vehicles, flying planes/VTOL/
hovercraft, swimming and underwater propulsion, zero-G EVA movement, riding
animal mounts, skateboarding with a trick system, and piloting a boat or
submersible. The world exists to exercise the controller; the controller is
the centerpiece of the project, not the world.

STYLE
HDRP pipeline. Open-zone, high-fidelity, physically simulated. Every vehicle,
creature, and player form uses one unified "Control Surface Stack" that
dynamically remaps inputs based on the current mode (ground, air, water,
zero-G, riding, driving, flying, swimming, skating, climbing). Visually
grounded, mechanically dense — the aesthetic is secondary to the feel of each
mode's physics.

CORE PREMISE
The player seamlessly switches between many locomotion modes inside one large
open-zone test environment: mountain road, open field, a lake with an
underwater cave, open airspace, a zero-G test chamber, a skate park, a
vehicle garage, and a combat arena. Every mode must have full logic, not a
stub: input reading, a state machine, movement vectors, physics forces,
animation parameters, camera rig binding, mode transitions, and sub-mode
transitions (for example a plane's landing gear, flaps, and rudder).

PLAYER
The player occupies one of several controller modes at a time, switching
through a shared Mode Manager and a unified Input Action Map:

- On-Foot Controller: WASD/analog-stick movement, sprint, crouch, slide,
  vault, wall-run, parkour auto-mantle, a combo-based melee system, a ranged
  weapon with ADS/recoil/reload/fire-modes, free-look camera with a
  shoulder-swap.
- Driving Controller (cars, bikes, off-road): torque-curve acceleration,
  suspension compression, traction control, a drifting system with angle
  detection and counter-steer, handbrake, headlights/signals/hazards, a
  chase cam and a hood cam.
- Flying Controller (planes, VTOL, hovercraft): pitch/yaw/roll, flaps,
  rudder, landing gear, a throttle curve, stall detection, a VTOL hover
  mode, cockpit and chase cameras.
- Swimming/Underwater Controller: buoyancy, drag, six-axis movement, swim
  strokes versus underwater thrusters, a free-float camera.
- Zero-G EVA Controller: full 6DOF thruster movement, rotational inertia, a
  stabilization toggle, a free-orbit camera.
- Riding Controller (animal mounts): an acceleration curve, jump and double
  jump, mount stamina, and attack modes while mounted.
- Skateboard Controller: a trick system (ollie/kickflip/grind-style inputs),
  grinding rails, manual balance, flip tricks, a dynamic follow camera.
- Boat/Submersible Controller: water displacement, wake simulation, rudder
  steering, and dive planes for the submersible.

Every mode shares one Input Action Map (movement in 2D and 3D, look, mode
switch, sub-mode switch, ten general action slots, trick combos, vehicle
subsystem inputs, weapon subsystem inputs, camera subsystem inputs), one
multi-mode camera rig (mode-specific follow logic, dynamic FOV, shake
profiles, auto-targeting in combat, vehicle-specific damping), and one set of
physics helpers shared across modes: Rigidbody or CharacterController logic,
forces/impulses/drag/damping, ground detection, water detection, air
detection, and zero-G detection, each applying surface-type modifiers.

ENTITIES
- PlayerAvatar: the on-foot human form, parkour and combat capable (kind:
  player, model: player avatar).
- OffRoadCar: the player-drivable off-road car with torque-curve acceleration
  and traction control (kind: vehicle, model: off-road car).
- TrailBike: the player-drivable dirt bike with a drift/counter-steer system
  (kind: vehicle, model: trail bike).
- VTOLCraft: the player-flyable VTOL aircraft with flaps, rudder, landing
  gear, and a hover mode (kind: vehicle, model: vtol aircraft).
- Hovercraft: the player-flyable hovercraft (kind: vehicle, model:
  hovercraft).
- PatrolBoat: the player-drivable surface boat with wake simulation (kind:
  vehicle, model: patrol boat).
- Submersible: the player-drivable submersible with dive planes (kind:
  vehicle, model: submersible).
- MountBeast: the rideable animal mount with a stamina system (kind: npc,
  model: mount beast).
- Skateboard: the player's skateboard with a trick and grind system (kind:
  vehicle, model: skateboard).
- MountainRoad: the mountain road driving section of the test zone (kind:
  terrain, model: mountain road).
- OpenField: the open-field on-foot and parkour section (kind: terrain,
  model: open field).
- UnderwaterCave: the lake and underwater cave swimming section (kind:
  terrain, model: underwater cave).
- ZeroGChamber: the zero-G EVA test chamber (kind: terrain, model: zero-g
  chamber).
- SkatePark: the skate park trick section (kind: terrain, model: skate
  park).
- VehicleGarage: the vehicle garage where the player switches between
  drivable vehicles (kind: prop, model: vehicle garage).
- CombatArena: the combat arena for melee, ranged, and mounted combat
  testing (kind: terrain, model: combat arena).
- GrindRail: a grindable rail in the skate park (kind: prop, model: grind
  rail).

OBJECTIVE
There is no win/lose condition — the objective is full mode coverage. The
player must be able to enter every one of the eight locomotion modes (on
foot, driving, flying, swimming, zero-G, riding, skateboarding, boating),
switch between them through the Mode Manager without breaking physics state,
and exercise every listed subsystem (parkour, combat, vehicle handling,
flight subsystems, buoyancy, thrusters, mount stamina, trick scoring, wake
simulation) at least once across the test zone's eight areas (mountain road,
open field, lake/underwater cave, airspace, zero-G chamber, skate park,
vehicle garage, combat arena).
