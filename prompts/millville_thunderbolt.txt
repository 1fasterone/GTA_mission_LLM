TITLE
Millville Thunderbolt

GENRE
A fully 3D arcade motorcycle racing game. Genre is racing — a technical road-course
circuit race on a single reproduced track layout, ridden against rival racers.

STYLE
Grounded modern motorsport realism: sunlit asphalt, painted curbing, chain-link
paddock fencing, canvas team tents, low-poly leaning toward photoreal.

CORE PREMISE
The player rides a sport motorcycle around the Millville circuit, a technical road
course reproduced from a reference track map: a long front straight past the
start/finish gantry into a tightening infield esses/chicane complex, a hairpin
loop-back section that doubles the circuit over itself, and a sweeping outer run
that carries riders past the trackside paddock before funneling back onto the front
straight. Three AI rival riders share the grid. First bike across the start/finish
line after three laps wins.

TRACK LAYOUT (reproduced from reference map)
- Front straight: wide, start/finish gantry at the midpoint, leads into turn 1.
- Infield esses (turns 1-5): a tightening S-shaped chicane complex requiring
  quick left-right-left lean transitions.
- Hairpin loop-back (turns 6-9): a switchback that folds the circuit back on
  itself, tightest corners on the course, apex markers at each hairpin.
- Sweeping back section (turns 10-11): high-speed sweepers that run past the
  paddock fence line and merge back onto the front straight.
- Full perimeter boundary: continuous curbing and barrier fencing tracing the
  entire outside and inside edge of the ribbon, matching the reference map's
  outline exactly — no gaps in the boundary.

PLAYER
A rider on a sport motorcycle. Uses a vehicle_bike controller (throttle, brake,
lean/steer, countersteer balance). Rigidbody wheel-collider physics, base=bike
model (motorcycle, engine=1, tank=1, pedals=0). Third-person chase camera that
banks with lean angle through corners.

ENTITIES
- PlayerBike: the player's sport motorcycle (kind: vehicle, model_hint "sport
  motorcycle"), grid slot 1 on the front straight facing turn 1.
- RivalStormrider: an aggressive AI rival motorcycle that brakes late into the
  hairpins (kind: vehicle, model_hint "sport motorcycle"), grid slot 2.
- RivalIronclad: a steady AI rival motorcycle that carries corner speed through
  the esses (kind: vehicle, model_hint "sport motorcycle"), grid slot 3.
- RivalNightfall: a fast-on-straights AI rival motorcycle that draws even on the
  back sweepers (kind: vehicle, model_hint "sport motorcycle"), grid slot 4.
- CircuitTrack: the Millville circuit road surface (kind: track, model_hint
  "race track technical circuit"), laid out per the TRACK LAYOUT section above.
- BoundaryWall: curbing and barrier fencing ringing the full circuit perimeter,
  inside and outside edge, with no gaps (kind: wall, model_hint "barrier").
- StartFinishBanner: the start/finish gantry banner spanning the front straight
  (kind: decoration, model_hint "banner checkpoint").
- PaddockTent: three team support tents/canopies clustered in the paddock area
  off the back sweepers, behind the boundary fence (kind: prop, model_hint
  "paddock tent"), count 3.
- PaddockFence: chain-link fencing separating the paddock area from the racing
  surface (kind: wall, model_hint "chain-link fence").
All four motorcycles should resolve to real sport-motorcycle models and be spawned
on their correct grid slots on the front straight, facing turn 1, not overlapping.

AI
Rival motorcycles use waypoint_follow racing-line AI around the circuit, following
the TRACK LAYOUT's corner sequence with rubber-band catch-up so no rival falls out
of contention.

SCENES
- Main Menu: play, best times, settings, quit.
- Millville Circuit: the single reproduced track — grid start, three-lap race,
  paddock visible off the back section.
Fade transition between menu and circuit.

HUD
Speedometer, lap counter (lap 1/3), race position (1st/4), and a minimap tracing
the full circuit outline including the hairpin loop-back. Countdown "3-2-1-GO!"
banner at the start/finish line when the race begins.

AUDIO
Revving four-stroke engine loop per bike (louder for the player, spatial for
rivals), tire squeal on hard lean through the hairpins, a trackside crowd murmur
near the paddock, and an upbeat racing music loop. 3D positional engine audio so
rivals are audible before they're seen.

FEATURES
Save/load best lap time (json_file). Pause menu (resume, restart, quit to menu).
Results screen showing final finishing position after three laps.

VISUAL STYLE
URP with bloom and strong midday directional sunlight. Sunbaked asphalt with
faded racing line, painted curbing in alternating red/white, chain-link paddock
fencing, canvas tents in muted team colors.
