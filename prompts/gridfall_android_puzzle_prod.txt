TITLE
Gridfall

GENRE
A 2d game puzzle: a falling-block stacking puzzle for Android phones, portrait,
one thumb. There is no player character and no avatar — no one walks, drives or
is piloted. The player acts only by touching the blocks. No 3d scenery.

STYLE
Dark, calm, high contrast. A near-black background, a slightly lighter well and
saturated flat block colours with a thin lighter edge on every cell, so a stack
reads at arm's length on a phone. Readouts are flat dark cards with pale
numerals. Large type, generous spacing, no decoration that is not information.

CORE PREMISE
Pieces made of four cells fall into a ten-by-twenty well. The player slides and
rotates each one as it falls and locks it into the stack. Any row that fills
completely is cleared and everything above it drops down. Clearing rows scores,
every ten rows raises the level and speeds the fall, and the run ends when a
new piece has nowhere legal to sit.

ENTITIES
- FallingBlock: a single four-cell tetromino cell, flat coloured with a lighter
  edge, the thing the player touches and moves (kind: collectible, model_hint
  "cube block"), count 28.
- LockedBlock: a cell already locked into the stack, waiting for its row to
  fill (kind: collectible, model_hint "cube block"), count 24.
- WellFrame: the recessed ten-by-twenty well the blocks fall inside (kind:
  prop, model_hint "panel frame"), count 1.
- GhostOutline: the pale outline showing where the current piece will land
  (kind: prop, model_hint "outline square"), count 1.
- NextBox: the readout panel showing the two upcoming pieces (kind: prop,
  model_hint "panel card"), count 1.
- HoldBox: the readout panel holding the one piece set aside (kind: prop,
  model_hint "panel card"), count 1.
- ScoreReadout: the score, lines and level numerals (kind: prop, model_hint
  "panel card"), count 3.

MISSIONS
1. Reach Level Two — survive the opening speed and clear the first ten rows.
- Clear ten full rows out of the well (collect: FallingBlock x10)
- Keep the stack below the top of the well while you do it (survive)
2. Reach Level Three — the gravity timer shortens and the stack builds faster.
- Clear ten more full rows at the higher speed (collect: LockedBlock x10)
- Keep the stack below the top of the well again (survive)
3. Hold The Well — play on until a new piece has nowhere legal to sit.
- Stay alive as long as the pieces keep coming (survive)

SCENES
- Main — the well, the blocks falling into it, and the readout column beside
  it. This is where the whole game is played.
- MainMenu — the title over a still stack, with Play and Quit.

SCRIPTS
- GridLogic (custom) — owns the ten-by-twenty grid of cells and knows which are
  filled; spawns the next piece at the top; steps the active piece down one row
  on every tick of a gravity timer; refuses a move or rotation that would put a
  cell into a wall, the floor or an occupied cell; locks a piece that can fall
  no further; finds every full row, removes it and drops the rows above it;
  scores by how many rows went at once; raises the level and shortens the
  gravity interval every ten rows; ends the run when a new piece cannot be
  placed.
- TouchInput (custom) — reads swipes and taps and turns them into the moves:
  swipe left or right to shift a column, tap to rotate a quarter turn, swipe
  down to soft-drop, flick down to hard-drop, swipe up to send the piece to the
  Hold box.

AUDIO
- A quiet low drone under everything.
- A soft click when a piece shifts or rotates.
- A firmer thud when a piece locks.
- A bright ascending chime when a row clears.
- A four-note flourish when four rows clear at once.
- A short descending tone when the well tops out.

PLATFORM
Android phones only, targeting Android 12 and newer. Portrait orientation,
touch input only — no keyboard, no gamepad, no mouse.
