GENRE: racing
STYLE: third-person, fully 3d
CORE PREMISE: Outdoor motocross circuit racing on a pro national dirt track.

A fully 3d motocross racing game set on the Spring Creek national circuit, a
1.3-mile outdoor dirt track traced from the official 2025 track map. The player
rides a motocross bike from a gated start straight into the holeshot first
corner, then attacks a hillside course of sweeping berms, whooped straights,
tight switchback hairpins and a high double-loop section before crossing the
checkered finish line. Rival riders launch from pole positions on the same
start gate and fight for the racing line every lap.

Dusty summer daylight over packed brown dirt, trackside grandstands, sponsor
banners, safety flags and a creek winding past the far side of the valley.
Roaring engines, spectators cheering at the fence line. Lap timing, race
position tracking and a finish-line checkpoint decide the race.
