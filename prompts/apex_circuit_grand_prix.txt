A high-stakes illegal street race at Los Santos International Airport at night, in a
rolling thunderstorm. The player challenges Vice, a ruthless, unbeatable ace racer who
has never lost a circuit in his life — make Vice as brutal and punishing as possible,
the toughest rival in the city, top difficulty. Two more rival cars join Vice on the
grid. Three laps of the airport's figure-8 circuit, first across the finish line by
the hangars wins a big cash prize. Before the race starts, the player should be able
to open a settings menu to adjust the weather and Vice's difficulty.
