GENRE: off-road desert racing
STYLE: fully 3D, third-person chase camera, gritty photorealistic desert racing, sun-bleached ochre and dust palette
CORE PREMISE: six trophy trucks howl across a 12-mile loop of open desert in the Baja 250, launching off whoop sections and dry-wash crossings on long-travel suspension while the player claws from last starting position to the finish arch before the leaders escape.

BAJA 250 is a fully 3D off-road desert racing game for PC, chase-cam behind the player's own trophy truck. The course is a single sprawling desert loop — hard-packed silt flats, rutted whoop sections, a dry lakebed straightaway, and a rocky wash crossing — marked the whole way by ribbon-and-flag course markers, tire-stack barriers at the sharp switchbacks, and hay-bale chicanes where the track threads past the spectator pits. Three timing checkpoints along the loop bank the truck's split time, and a finish-line arch freezes the standings the instant the leader crosses it.

Every truck on the grid — the player's and the five rivals racing alongside — is built around exposed long-travel suspension: front double-wishbone A-arms and rear trailing arms swinging through three feet of wheel travel, paired with visible external-reservoir coilover shocks that compress hard on landing and rebound as the truck claws back onto the throttle. Hitting a whoop section at speed launches the truck into the air; a soft landing keeps momentum, a hard one bottoms the suspension out and scrubs speed. A damage system tracks suspension health from repeated hard landings and rival contact — three blown shocks end the run, and a pit-crew stop lets the player trade time for a suspension repair. A prize-winnings system lets the player spend race payouts between events on stiffer shocks, wider tires, and engine upgrades.

Rival trucks run adaptive racing AI: they pick their own line through the whoops, draft the leader on the lakebed straightaway, and nerf for position at the tire-stack switchbacks. A pit-crew NPC calls out tire and suspension status over the radio at each checkpoint. Particle VFX cover a rooster-tail of sand off the rear tires at speed, a dust cloud kicked up by every hard landing, and a spark shower when a truck's skid plate scrapes rock in the wash crossing. Dynamic lighting carries the race from a hazy dawn start under a pastel sky through a blazing midday sun that bakes the silt flats white and throws long hard-edged shadows off every truck, with heat-shimmer distortion rising off the dry lakebed. A Timeline-driven slow-motion sequence plays as the leader crosses the finish arch.

Render pipeline: URP with bloom for the midday sun and a volumetric dust layer hanging over the course, on Unity 6000.0. Recommended packages: Cinemachine for the chase camera, Input System, Timeline for the finish sequence, and Animation Rigging for the drivers' arms working the wheel over rough ground.
