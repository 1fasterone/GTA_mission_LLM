Title: Sky Piercer: 2.5D Ring Toss Challenge

Core Concept:  
A stylized 2.5D ring‑toss skill game where players throw rings toward pegs at varying distances, elevations, and motion patterns. The camera is fixed in a shallow side‑angle perspective (2.5D), giving depth without full 3D freedom. The gameplay loop emphasizes precision, timing, and environmental mastery.

Gameplay Specification
Player Actions
Tap/hold/drag to set throw angle, force, and spin.

Release to throw a ring along a physics‑driven arc.

Optional “perfect toss” slow‑motion window on high‑accuracy throws.

Rings

Rings can bounce, roll, or hook onto pegs depending on angle and force.

Variants:

Heavy Ring – slower arc, more stable

Feather Ring – floaty, high drag

Mag‑Ring – slight attraction to metallic pegs (rare power-up)

Targets (Pegs)
Static pegs at different depths (foreground, midground, background).

Moving pegs sliding horizontally or vertically.

Rotating pegs with timing windows.

Special pegs:

Glow Peg – bonus points

Shift Peg – teleports to a new depth after each hit

Wind Peg – emits gusts that alter ring trajectory

2.5D layered environment with parallax backgrounds.

Dynamic depth-of-field to emphasize target layers.

Background elements: floating lanterns, drifting clouds, gentle wind particles.

Level Structure
Tiered lanes: near, mid, far.

Increasing difficulty:

More moving pegs

Scoring
