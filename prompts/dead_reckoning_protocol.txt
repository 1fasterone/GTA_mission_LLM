TITLE
Dead Reckoning Protocol

GENRE
A 3D third-person infiltration-and-extraction mission. Open-world action game.

STYLE
URP pipeline, grounded modern-military aesthetic, moody dusk lighting over an
industrial coastal fuel depot.

CORE PREMISE
The player is a lone operative who switches between three locomotion modes at
runtime through a shared mode manager: on-foot infiltration, driving a
getaway car between compound sections, and flying a VTOL aircraft out through
hostile airspace at the end. The player can get in and out of the car and the
VTOL freely; each mode has its own full controller — on-foot movement and
combat, driving physics, and flight physics with flaps, rudder, and
retractable landing gear for a tight rooftop touchdown.

PLAYER
An on-foot infiltration specialist trained in parkour, wall-run traversal,
vault and mantle movement, and melee combat with guards. Once inside the
depot, the operative gets into the getaway car to cross to the hangar. At the
hangar, the operative switches into the VTOL Interceptor to fly out, using
flaps and landing gear for a precision landing on the extraction rooftop
before final takeoff.

ENTITIES
- Operative: the on-foot player form, parkour and combat capable (kind:
  player, model: operative).
- GetawaySedan: the player-drivable getaway car (kind: vehicle, model:
  sedan).
- VTOLInterceptor: the player-flyable VTOL aircraft with flaps, rudder, and
  retractable landing gear (kind: vehicle, model: vtol aircraft).
- PatrolGuard: armed guards defending the depot (kind: enemy, count: 6,
  model: patrol guard).
- FuelDepotYard: the industrial fuel depot's inner yard (kind: terrain,
  model: fuel depot).
- SupplyCrate: a crate holding a fuel cell the player must collect (kind:
  pickup, count: 3, model: supply crate).
- Engineer: the stranded engineer the player must escort to the extraction
  pad (kind: npc, model: engineer).
- ExtractionPad: the rooftop landing zone (kind: terrain, model: extraction
  pad).
- IntelDocument: an optional intel document hidden in a side office (kind:
  pickup, model: intel document).

OBJECTIVE
First, reach the depot's inner yard on foot past the loading dock. Then
collect the three fuel cells from the supply crates scattered around the
yard. Next, eliminate the six patrol guards defending the hangar. After that,
hold the extraction rooftop for 60 seconds while the VTOL's engines spin up —
reinforcements are inbound and the player must survive the assault. Finally,
escort the engineer aboard the VTOL and take off. Optionally, and only once
inside the yard, the player may collect the intel document hidden in the
depot's side office for a bonus reward — it is not required to complete the
mission.
