TITLE
Harbor Light Salvage

GENRE
A fully 3D third-person stealth action-adventure game. Genre is stealth — the player
explores a decommissioned harbor on foot, sneaks past patrolling scavenger drones,
recovers salvage, and completes a chain of objectives to relight the lighthouse.

STYLE
Weathered industrial coastal realism at dusk: rust-streaked shipping containers, wet
concrete quays, sodium dock lamps throwing long orange pools, cold blue sea haze
rolling in off the water. Low-poly leaning toward grounded, not stylized-cartoon.

CORE PREMISE
The harbor's lighthouse has been dark for a decade and the tide is bringing wrecks in
onto the rocks. The player is a salvage diver contracted to walk the quays, pull three
power cells out of the wrecked freighter, the flooded pump house, and the crane yard,
carry them back to the lighthouse generator room, and bring the beacon back to life
before the night tide crests. Scavenger drones patrol the container stacks and defend
the cells. The harbormaster, an old dockhand who never left, radios the player between
objectives and hands over the tools.

PLAYER
A salvage diver on foot in a dive suit. Third-person humanoid character controller —
walk, sprint, jump, crouch through low pipe runs. Rigidbody capsule physics with a
ground check. Third-person orbit camera that follows behind the shoulder and collides
with the container stacks instead of clipping through them.

AI
Scavenger drones use NavMesh patrol between the container stacks, switch to chase when
the player enters their detection radius, and return to patrol when the player breaks
line of sight. Run them from a behavior tree — a Selector over Chase, Investigate, and
Patrol branches. Waves of extra drones spawn from the crane yard each time a power cell
is pulled. Staying out of a drone's cone keeps the player undetected.

SCENES
- Main Menu: play, continue, settings, quit.
- Harbor Quays: the main playable scene — lighthouse, quays, container yard, crane
  yard, pump house, beached freighter, drones on patrol.
Fade transition between the menu and the harbor.

HUD
Health bar with a shield segment, a power-cell counter (0/3), a scrap counter, an
objective banner naming the current objective, and an interaction prompt that appears
when the player is in range of a cell, a crate, or the harbormaster.

FEATURES
Objective tracker chaining the four objectives (three cells, then relight the beacon)
with completion events. Inventory as a hotbar holding the cells and collected scrap.
Health system with a regenerating shield over flat health. Save/load progress to a
json_file, with a checkpoint written each time a power cell is delivered. Pause menu
with resume, restart, and quit to menu. Interactables: the power cells and salvage
crates are pickups, the generator room door is a door, and the beacon switch is a
lever. Branching dialogue with the harbormaster between objectives, with barks from
the drones when they spot the player.

AUDIO
Looping harbor ambience — waves against the quay wall, mooring ropes creaking, a
distant buoy bell. Spatial drone rotor whine so patrolling drones are audible before
they are seen. A metallic clunk when a power cell seats into the generator. A low
tense music bed that swells when drones give chase. The lighthouse beacon spinning up
with a rising electrical hum at the finale.

DIALOGUE
The harbormaster speaks his lines aloud — a weary, gravelly old dockhand voice.

VISUAL STYLE
URP with bloom, fog, and dusk-orange directional light low on the horizon. Rust and
salt-stained metal, wet reflective concrete, sodium lamp pools, cold blue sea haze.
Dissolve shader on drones when destroyed, hit-flash on the player when damaged.
