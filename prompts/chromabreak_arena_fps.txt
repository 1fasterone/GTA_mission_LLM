TITLE
Chromabreak

GENRE
A fully 3d first-person shooter. Genre is fps — a fast, close-quarters arena
shooter with real gunplay and melee. The player holds a weapon in view, aims
down the centre of the screen, sprints and strafes constantly, and fights waves
of enemies that close in from every side. Movement is quick: the fight is won by
never standing still.

STYLE
Saturated prismatic neon. The arena is a glass-and-chrome fighting pit lit by
banks of coloured floodlights — hot magenta key light, cyan rim light, an amber
accent from the pit floor, and a deep indigo background. Every surface is either
a glossy metallic panel with low roughness, a cel shaded toon-outlined enemy
shell, or an emissive neon strip. High contrast, hard shadows, bloom on every
light source. Nothing in this game is grey.

CORE PREMISE
The player is a chrome-suited pit fighter dropped into the Chromabreak arena.
Waves of hostile shard constructs spawn from vents around the pit, hunt the
player down and attack in melee range. The player breaks them with the pulse
rifle and a fast melee punch, banks the colour shards they drop, and survives
wave after wave. Each wave is faster and denser than the last. Clearing the
final wave of a round opens the vault door to the next round.

PLAYER
A chrome-suited pit fighter in first person. Uses a first_person controller —
CharacterController-based walk, sprint and strafe with a short hop, held to the
pit floor by gravity. Mouse look drives the aim, WASD moves, shift sprints, and
the whole controller is tuned quick: a high move speed, a high sprint
multiplier, and a responsive look sensitivity so a 180 degree turn is one flick.
Left click fires the pulse rifle down the crosshair with a muzzle flash, a
recoil kick, a spent-casing spark and a short cooldown; limited ammo per clip
with a reload. Right click throws a fast melee punch that knocks a construct
back. The player animation rig is humanoid with an idle, a walk cycle, a sprint,
a fire gesture, a punch and a hurt reaction.

AI
Every ShardConstruct uses a ground chase AI: it climbs out of a VentPillar,
holds a short idle while it acquires the player, then hunts the player down at
speed, closes to melee range and attacks. It can be knocked back and briefly
stunned by a punch or a rifle hit. Constructs patrol between vents when they
have no detection on the player and go aggro the moment they see one. Elite
constructs acquire faster, hit harder and take more shots. Waves spawn from the
spawner on a timer with a short lull between them; the construct count and speed
step up every wave.

SCENES
- Main Menu: start run, high scores, settings, quit.
- Chromabreak Arena: the fight scene — one glass pit, six glowing vent pillars
  around the rim, chrome barriers scattered for cover, coloured floodlight banks
  overhead, and constructs inbound from every vent.

MISSIONS
- Round One — Break the First Wave: defeat 10 ShardConstructs, collect 5
  ColourShards, and reach the vault door alive.
- Round Two — Hold the Pit: survive the second wave for 90 seconds and defeat
  the elite construct that spawns with it.

COLOUR REQUIREMENTS
These are hard requirements, not mood notes. The game must be loud with colour.
- Fill color_palette with the arena's real colours and nothing grey or
  near-black: primary hot magenta, secondary electric cyan, accent amber, trim
  white, background deep indigo. Every one a valid #RRGGBB hex.
- Author named materials for every distinct surface the concept describes — the
  chrome suit, the glass pit floor, the crystalline construct shell, the
  emissive neon strip, the amber vent glow, the chrome barrier — each with its
  own strong hex colour and its own smoothness and metallic roughness value. No
  two materials may share a colour.
- Author shaders for the looks a plain material cannot give: a toon ramp with a
  hard black outline on the construct shells, and a scrolling neon hologram
  scanline on the vault door. Give each one a runtime-driven property so the
  colour actually moves — a colour cycle, a pulse, a flash on hit.
- Lighting is coloured, not white: a magenta key, a cyan rim, an amber floor
  bounce, an indigo ambient. Bloom on every emissive surface.

PARTICLE REQUIREMENTS
These are hard requirements too. Every important event in this game throws
particles.
- Muzzle flash: a bright magenta spark burst at the rifle muzzle on every shot,
  with a short smoke puff and a spent-casing trail.
- Impact: a cyan spark and debris burst wherever a rifle bolt hits a construct
  or a wall, scaled by damage.
- Construct break: an explosion of crystalline shard debris, a dust puff and a
  bright colour flash when a construct dies.
- Shard pickup: a small amber sparkle that rises and fades when a ColourShard is
  collected.
- Vent glow: a looping ambient particle plume rising from each VentPillar, on
  for the whole scene.
- Every effect above is a real particle system, object-pooled, played on the
  event and auto-destroyed after it finishes — except the looping vent plume.

HUD
Centre crosshair that flares on a hit, suit integrity bar, ammo counter with
clip and reserve, current wave number, colour-shard score, an objective readout
for the current round, a wave-incoming banner, a damage vignette at the screen
edge when the player is hit, and a game-over screen showing waves survived and
constructs broken.

DIALOGUE
The Quartermaster talks to the player over the intercom in plain text — a
briefing line before the first wave, a warning line when the elite construct
spawns, and a congratulation line on a cleared round. Text only, no voice
acting.

AUDIO
A punchy pulse-rifle crack with a synth tail, a reload clack, a heavy melee
thud, a glassy shatter when a construct breaks, a bright chime on a shard
pickup, a low warning drone as a wave closes in, and a driving synth music bed
that speeds up wave by wave. Spatial audio on every construct so the player can
hear which vent the next one is coming from.

FEATURES
Save/load high scores and best wave (json_file). Pause menu (resume, restart,
quit to menu). Game-over screen with a restart option. A fade transition between
the menu and the arena.

VISUAL STYLE
URP, stylized, high saturation. Bloom on the rifle bolts, muzzle flash, neon
strips and floodlights. Hard coloured key light, strong rim separation, deep
indigo background, long hard shadows across the glass floor.
