A hijack gone wrong on the highway overpass. The player boxes in a cash truck and
has to hold the crossing while cracking it open. One flare gun is the player's
whole toolkit for calling in help — pop it and a chase car full of backup roars
in from the on-ramp, and popping the same flare again is also how the player
signals a jacked delivery van to peel in and take the cash the second it's
loaded, so nothing gets left behind when the highway patrol shows up.
