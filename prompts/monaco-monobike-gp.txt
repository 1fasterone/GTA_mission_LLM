TITLE
Monaco Monowheel Grand Prix

GENRE
A fully 3d third-person racing game. Genre is racing — a timed circuit race
around a real street course: one player machine, three AI rivals, three laps
through the harbour, the hairpin and the tunnel, fastest total time wins.

STYLE
Victorian steampunk motorsport on the Riviera. Brass-and-iron single-wheel
monobikes — the rider sits inside one enormous spoked wheel — hammering
through the narrow Monte Carlo streets. Polished brass fittings, riveted iron
plate, leaking steam, oil smoke and coal soot against pale stone barrier walls
and Mediterranean sun. Elegant, grimy, mechanical.

CORE PREMISE
The player rides a Victorian monobike around the full Monaco Grand Prix street
circuit — a single one-piece racetrack model with its road surface and barrier
walls already built, 291 by 660 metres with 19 metres of elevation change from
the harbour front up through the hill section. Three rival monobike racers set
the pace. The player must complete three laps, keeping the machine off the
barrier walls through the hairpin, and post the fastest total time. Clipping a
wall costs speed and vents steam; a clean racing line through the tunnel builds
a boost of pressure.

PLAYER
The Victorian monobike — a single giant spoked wheel with the rider slung
inside it. Uses a vehicle_ground controller tuned for a narrow street circuit
(throttle, brake, steering, handbrake) with rigidbody physics, a low centre of
mass and pronounced lean into corners. Third-person chase camera behind and
slightly above the wheel that pulls back with speed.

ENTITIES
- MonacoCircuit: the full one-piece Monaco Grand Prix street circuit, road
  surface and barrier walls in a single model, serving as the ground the race
  is run on (kind: track, model: monaco grand prix circuit).
- PlayerMonobike: the player's Victorian monobike — one enormous spoked wheel
  with the rider inside (kind: vehicle, model: victorian monobike).
- RivalMonobikeOne: the first AI rival's Victorian monobike (kind: vehicle,
  model: victorian monobike).
- RivalMonobikeTwo: the second AI rival's Victorian monobike (kind: vehicle,
  model: victorian monobike).
- RivalMonobikeThree: the third AI rival's Victorian monobike (kind: vehicle,
  model: victorian monobike).
- StartFinishBanner: the start and finish gantry over the pit straight
  (kind: prop).
- SectorMarker: a timing marker at each sector split around the lap
  (kind: prop).

OBJECTIVE
Complete three laps of the Monaco circuit and finish ahead of the three rival
monobikes. A lap counter and a running lap time are shown on screen. Hitting a
barrier wall scrubs speed. The race ends when the player crosses the finish
line on the third lap; finishing first wins.
