HALCYON DRIFT — a 3D third-person survival-exploration game for PC.

The player is a salvage diver who wakes from cryosleep aboard the Halcyon, a derelict
generation ship adrift between stars. The reactor is failing, maintenance drones have
turned hostile, and a fractured ship AI whispers through the comms. Explore the decks,
restore power, talk to the few surviving crew, and reach the escape pod before the hull
loses pressure. Heavy on atmosphere: groaning metal, flickering lights, drifting debris,
and spatial 3D sound.

GAME TYPE: 3D third-person, walk locomotion, gravity on most decks.
PLATFORM: PC.  RENDER PIPELINE: URP (volumetric fog, bloom for holograms, color grading).
UNITY VERSION: 6000.0.  VISUAL STYLE: realistic, cold industrial blues with amber alarms.

SCENES (7 total, each a Unity scene, with fade/load transitions between them):
1. MainMenu      — title over a slow pan of the drifting ship, Start / Load / Quit
2. CryoBay       — waking zone, tutorial, first flickering lights and ambient hum
3. Concourse     — central hub deck, surviving crew NPCs, shops/lockers, branching halls
4. ReactorCore   — hazardous deck, steam vents, radiation, the power-restore objective
5. MaintenanceWarrens — dark crawlspaces, drone ambushes, low visibility
6. Bridge        — the ship AI confrontation, story climax
7. EscapePod      — ending sequence, launch and drift to safety

NARRATIVE — eight story beats:
1. Cold Wake     — player exits the cryopod for the first time (on_start)
2. First Whisper — the ship AI speaks over the comms (on_enter_zone)
3. Crew Found    — player meets the first surviving crew member (on_pickup)
4. Power Low     — reactor output drops, alarms escalate (on_timer)
5. Drone Swarm   — maintenance drones attack from the warrens (on_enemy_death)
6. Core Breach   — the reactor deck floods with coolant (on_enter_zone)
7. AI Reveal     — the AI's true intent is exposed on the bridge (manual)
8. Drift Free    — the escape pod launches (on_enter_zone)

SCRIPTS — every system the game needs:
- PlayerController   (player)       third-person walk/sprint/jump, mantle, crouch in vents
- O2Manager         (custom)        oxygen + hull-pressure countdown, refill at O2 stations,
                                     low-O2 vignette + heartbeat, death on empty
                                     (reach the player via GameContext.Instance.Get<PlayerHealth>())
- PowerGrid         (custom)        deck-by-deck power restoration, doors/lights gated on power
- DroneAI           (enemy)         NavMesh patrol -> alert -> charge -> self-destruct,
                                     reacts to the player's flashlight and noise
- ShipAI            (custom)        the fractured AI: triggers comms barks, manipulates doors
- UnderlightCamera  (camera)        Cinemachine third-person, lazy follow, slight handheld sway
- GameManager       (game_manager)  state machine (Menu/Playing/Paused/Dead/Win), score
                                     (data shards), lives (respawns), scene transitions, save
- UIManager         (ui_manager)    HUD: O2 bar, hull-pressure meter, objective marker,
                                     ammo/flashlight battery, pause + game-over panels
- AudioManager      (custom)        spatial audio pool, zone ambience crossfade, plays cues by id
- HealthSystem      (health)        player + drone HP, damage events, death callbacks
- DataShardPickup   (collectible)   pickup triggers a story-beat line and score
- InventoryManager  (custom)        slots: keycards, tools, O2 canisters, flashlight cells
- DialogueManager   (dialogue_manager) story-beat subtitles + voice-over triggers
- MaterialPalette   (material_palette) runtime industrial palette application
- ProceduralHull    (level_generator) ProBuilder corridors, bulkheads, debris fields

ENTITIES:
- Player: salvage diver in a pressure suit, humanoid rig
- Enemy:  maintenance drone, generic rig, hover + charge animations (count ~4, scatter)
- NPC:    surviving crew — a quartermaster (vendor), an engineer (quest giver), a medic
          (these are VOICED NPCs with spoken dialog barks and quest lines)
- Collectible: data shard (glowing cyan cube)
- Collectible: O2 refill station (wall-mounted)
- Prop:   flickering ceiling lights, steam vents, holographic signage

VOICED NPCs — the crew speak aloud (this needs the npc_dialog microgen):
- Quartermaster: gruff vendor, trades salvage for gear, English
- Engineer: anxious quest giver, sends the player to the reactor, English
- Medic: calm, warns about radiation, English

PARTICLE / VFX (this needs the vfx microgen):
- impact sparks when drones are hit or self-destruct
- steam bursts from ruptured vents
- electrical arcs on damaged panels
- a screen-fade flash on power surges

AUDIO — twelve cues for a rich spatial soundscape (Stable Audio 3):
- hull_groan          ambient  looping  deep metal stress and creak of a dying ship
- reactor_hum         ambient  looping  unstable low-frequency power drone, rises with danger
- cryobay_drip        ambient  looping  condensation drips and distant fans
- comms_static        ambient  looping  broken intercom hiss with fragments of speech
- drone_alert         sfx               sharp servo whine and target-lock chirp
- drone_explode       sfx               metallic burst and shrapnel scatter
- o2_alarm            sfx               rising warning cadence, low oxygen
- power_surge         sfx               electrical crack and capacitor whump
- data_shard_pickup   sfx               crystalline chime with digital confirm
- door_seal           sfx               heavy pneumatic bulkhead slam
- bridge_theme        music    looping  tense synth orchestral build, AI confrontation
- escape_launch_sting music             triumphant swell as the pod launches
