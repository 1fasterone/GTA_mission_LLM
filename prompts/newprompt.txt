EMBERFALL KEEP — a 3D third-person action-adventure for PC.

The player is the last knight of a mountain keep under siege by a fire cult that has
broken the outer wall. Fight through burning courtyards and collapsing towers, protect
the wounded garrison, and reach the chapel bell before the keep falls to ash. Heavy on
weight and impact: ringing steel, roaring fire, desperate shouting, and a tolling bell
that marks how much time is left.

GAME TYPE: 3D third-person, walk locomotion, gravity throughout.
PLATFORM: PC.  RENDER PIPELINE: URP (bloom for fire, volumetric smoke, warm god-rays).
UNITY VERSION: 6000.0.  VISUAL STYLE: gritty medieval realism, firelight orange against
cold stone blue.

SCENES (6 total, each a Unity scene, with fade transitions between them):
1. MainMenu       — title over a burning keep silhouette, Start / Load / Quit
2. OuterCourtyard — the breach, tutorial combat, first cultists pour through
3. GreatHall      — central hub, wounded soldiers, the quartermaster, branching stairs
4. CollapsingTower — vertical platforming + combat, structural collapse hazards
5. InnerSanctum   — the cult's ritual chamber, the strongest enemies, boss build-up
6. ChapelBell     — ending sequence, ring the bell, siege lifted

NARRATIVE — seven story beats:
1. Wall Falls      — the breach opens, player takes up arms (on_start)
2. First Blood     — player kills their first cultist (on_enemy_death)
3. Wounded Found   — player finds the wounded garrison in the Great Hall (on_pickup)
4. Tower Cracks    — the Collapsing Tower begins to give way (on_timer)
5. Near Death      — player drops below critical health for the first time (on_damage)
6. Ritual Exposed  — the cult's true purpose is revealed in the Inner Sanctum (manual)
7. Bell Tolls      — the chapel bell rings, the siege lifts (on_enter_zone)

SCRIPTS — every system the game needs:
- PlayerController  (player)         third-person walk/sprint/jump, block, heavy/light attack
- SiegeClock        (custom)         countdown to the keep falling, escalates enemy waves
                                     (reach the player via GameContext.Instance.Get<PlayerHealth>())
- GarrisonMorale    (custom)         wounded-soldiers-saved counter, gates the ending
- CultistAI         (enemy)          NavMesh patrol -> alert -> charge -> attack, flees on fire
- UnderlightCamera  (camera)         Cinemachine third-person, combat-tightened FOV on lock
- GameManager       (game_manager)   state machine (Menu/Playing/Paused/Dead/Win), score
                                     (cultists routed), lives (respawns at last brazier), save
- UIManager         (ui_manager)     HUD: health bar, stamina, morale counter, siege-clock timer,
                                     objective marker, pause + game-over panels
- AudioManager      (custom)         combat audio pool, zone ambience crossfade, plays cues by id
- HealthSystem      (health)         player + cultist HP, damage events, death callbacks, healing
- HealingShrinePickup (collectible)  pickup triggers a heal + a story-beat line
- InventoryManager  (custom)         slots: sword, shield, torches, healing draughts
- DialogueManager   (dialogue_manager) story-beat subtitles + voice-over triggers
- MaterialPalette   (material_palette) runtime firelit-stone palette application
- ProceduralKeep    (level_generator) ProBuilder courtyards, towers, collapsing debris

ENTITIES:
- Player: knight in scarred plate armor, humanoid rig
- Enemy:  fire cultist, generic rig, charge + ranged-throw animations (count ~4, scatter)
- NPC:    garrison survivors — a wounded sergeant (quest giver), the quartermaster (vendor),
          a field medic
          (these are VOICED NPCs with spoken dialog barks and quest lines)
- Collectible: healing shrine (glowing warm brazier)
- Collectible: torch cache (wall-mounted)
- Prop:   collapsing support beams, burning banners, cracked stone archways

VOICED NPCs — the garrison speaks aloud (this needs the npc_dialog microgen):
- Sergeant: hoarse quest giver, rallies the player to the tower, English
- Quartermaster: gruff vendor, trades supplies for salvaged gear, English
- Medic: urgent, warns about the wounded's dwindling time, English

- falling debris and dust when the tower cracks
