TITLE: SkyRing Descent
GENRE: Third-person 3D game, skydiving freefall arcade, ring-gate score attack, PC
STYLE: Stylized realism — hard sun above a cloud deck, altitude haze, neon ring gates, streaming wind vapour off the jumpsuit.

CORE PREMISE
The player is a competition skydiver flying gate runs. Each round begins aboard a
turboprop jump plane at 4000m; the player exits the open door, freefalls, and threads a
chain of glowing ring gates suspended down the whole drop corridor. Gates are stacked at
descending altitudes, so the run is one continuous vertical line rather than a course on
the ground. Miss a gate and the chain multiplier resets. Below the last gate the player
deploys the canopy and steers onto a bullseye landing pad in the drop zone. Pull altitude
is 1200m, the hard deck is 600m, and the reserve fires automatically below 300m.

SCENES (4 total)
MainMenu — animated title over a slow cloud deck, Start / Career / Quit
JumpRun — interior of the turboprop, door open, altimeter counting up to exit height
RingCorridor — the main gameplay drop: gate chain, rival divers, altitude haze
DropZoneLanding — canopy flight and the bullseye landing pad in the arena

NARRATIVE — four story beats
Exit Called — the jumpmaster calls the exit and the player leaves the plane (on_start)
First Chain — three gates threaded back to back without a miss (on_enter_zone)
Rival Cut — a rival diver tracks across the corridor and steals a gate (on_timer)
Bullseye — the player plants the canopy dead centre on the pad (on_enter_zone)

SCRIPTS
- FreefallController (player) body-attitude freefall control: track, delta, sit and flare
  poses; drag scales with body angle; terminal velocity plateau; altitude readout
- ParachuteSystem (custom) canopy deployment at the pull altitude, inflation physics,
  left/right riser steering, flare brake for the bullseye landing
- AltimeterManager (custom) live altitude from exit to touchdown, pull alarm at 1200m,
  hard deck warning at 600m, reserve auto-deploy below 300m
- RingGateChain (custom) tracks which gate is next, scores clean passes, keeps the chain
  multiplier, resets it on a miss, highlights the next gate in the corridor
- RivalDiver (enemy) AI divers track across the corridor on intercept lines and contest
  the gates; break off at pull altitude
- SkyCamera (camera) Cinemachine: chase mode in freefall, wide orbit under canopy, low
  cut for the landing
- GameManager (game_manager) state machine: Menu / JumpRun / Freefall / Canopy / Landed;
  score is gates chained plus landing accuracy
- UIManager (ui_manager) HUD: altimeter tape left, airspeed dial right, next-gate arrow,
  chain multiplier, pull warning flash
- HealthSystem (health) hard-deck breach and gate-frame collision cost a life

ENTITIES
- Player: competition skydiver in jumpsuit and helmet, humanoid rig, exits the jump plane
- NPC: turboprop jump plane, the aircraft the player exits at the top of the corridor
- Collectible: neon ring gate, a chain of them suspended down the corridor at descending
  altitudes, flown through for score
- Enemy: rival skydivers who contest the gates on intercept lines
- Collectible: bullseye landing pad on the drop zone floor
