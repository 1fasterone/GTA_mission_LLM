TITLE
Downtown Dual-Mode Heist

GENRE
A 3D open-zone crime game. The player explores a small downtown district on
foot and can get into a parked car to drive, switching between the two
locomotion modes at will through a shared control surface stack.

STYLE
Gritty modern city at dusk — concrete, neon signage, wet asphalt reflecting
streetlights. Grounded and a little run-down.

CORE PREMISE
The player starts on foot in a downtown district. A getaway car is parked
near the start. The player can walk around the district on foot, or get into
the car and drive, switching between the on-foot and driving locomotion modes
at any time with a single key press. Both modes are fully playable: on foot
the player can sprint; in the car the player can drive with a working nitro
boost. The objective is to reach a rooftop parking garage across town —
walking or driving both work, but driving is faster.

PLAYER
The player has ONE unified controller that switches between two locomotion
modes at runtime: on-foot (walking and sprinting) and driving (a real
physics car with a nitro boost). This is a "Control Surface Stack" —
locomotion_modes should be set to both on_foot and vehicle_car, with the
player starting on foot.

ENTITIES
- PlayerAvatar: the on-foot human form (kind: player, model: player avatar).
- GetawayCar: the parked car the player can drive (kind: vehicle, model:
  getaway car).
- ParkingGarage: the rooftop parking garage that is the destination (kind:
  building, model: parking garage).
- StreetBlock: a downtown street block making up the district (kind:
  terrain, model: city street block).

OBJECTIVE
Reach the rooftop parking garage across downtown, on foot or by driving the
getaway car. The player can switch between on-foot and driving at any time.
