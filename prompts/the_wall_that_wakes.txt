TITLE: The Wall That Wakes
GENRE: Third-person 3D game, vertical climbing, organic-horror puzzle, PC
STYLE: Wet stone and breathing flesh — bioluminescent veins under rock, fog that inhales,
muscle fibres where handholds should be.

CORE PREMISE
The player climbs a cliff that is alive. Every handhold is a sleeping creature, and the
wall itself is one organism made of thousands of them. Grip a Sleeper and it wakes: it
tenses, then shrugs you off after a few seconds. So the terrain IS the enemy, the enemy IS
the terrain, and the only safe route is over the parts still asleep. There is no ground
combat and no weapon — the player's only verbs are grip, release, and shift weight. Noise
wakes neighbours, so a panicked scramble cascades the whole face awake behind you. The
summit is a nerve cluster 900m up; reaching it ends the climb.

SCENES (3 total)
MainMenu — the face breathing in fog, title over a slow inhale, Start / Quit
TheFace — the main ascent: sleeping handholds, waking cascades, fog banks
NerveCluster — the summit organ, final sequence

NARRATIVE — four story beats
First Grip — the handhold under the player's palm opens an eye (on_start)
Cascade — a mistimed grab wakes a column of Sleepers below (on_enter_zone)
The Face Notices — the whole wall shifts once, deliberately (on_timer)
Nerve Cluster — the player reaches the summit organ (on_enter_zone)

SCRIPTS
- ClimbController (player) grip / release / weight-shift climbing on arbitrary surfaces;
  stamina drains while gripping a woken hold; fall recovery onto lower holds
- SleeperHold (enemy) a single living handhold: asleep, stirring, awake, shrugging states;
  wakes on grip or on noise from a neighbour; throws the player off when fully awake
- CascadeSystem (custom) propagates wake events between neighbouring Sleepers by distance,
  so noise spreads up and across the face
- WallOrganism (level_generator) the face itself: builds the climbable surface out of the
  Sleeper population and moves as one body when the whole face is awake
- FogBreath (custom) fog volumes that expand and contract with the wall's breathing cycle
- ClimbCamera (camera) Cinemachine: tight over-shoulder on the rock, pulls out on a fall
- GameManager (game_manager) state machine: Menu / Climbing / Falling / Summit / Consumed
- UIManager (ui_manager) HUD: stamina ring, grip indicator, wake-heat meter for the face

ENTITIES
- Player: a lone climber in a harness, humanoid rig, no weapons
- Enemy: Sleeper handholds — living creatures that ARE the climbable surface, hundreds of
  them covering the whole face, each one both terrain and hostile
- NPC: the Wall Organism itself, a single enormous creature that the entire cliff is part of
- Collectible: piton anchors the player can set into dormant flesh for a rest point
- Collectible: the nerve cluster at the summit, the goal of the climb
