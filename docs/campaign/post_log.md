# Post Log

> 5 lines per post. Filled in within 4 hours of the post, then revisited
> after 24 hours. The point is pattern recognition over weeks — what
> *types* of posts land.

## Template (copy this block per post)

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — <platform> — <channel or sub>
- Post: <title or first-line of caption>
- Format: <GIF / video / image / text>
- Initial reactions (4h): <upvotes / replies / DMs in>
- 24-hour reactions: <upvotes / replies / DMs>
- Surprise: <what worked or didn't that you didn't expect>
```

---

## Example entry (replace with your first real post)

### 2026-06-18 — Discord — Unity Official #self-promo
- Post: "Built a local scaffolder that turns one sentence into a Unity project or a playable GTA mission..."
- Format: 15s loop GIF
- Initial reactions (4h): 22 reactions, 6 thread replies, 2 DMs
- 24-hour reactions: 31 reactions, 11 thread replies, 4 DMs
- Surprise: All 4 DMs were Unity devs, not GTA modders — the GTA framing didn't draw the GTA crowd here. Re-emphasize Unity for this server next time.
